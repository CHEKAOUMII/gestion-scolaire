# Verdant Calm

An eye-friendly theme engineered for zero visual strain during extended digital use. Warm undertones throughout suppress blue-light, green-dominant primaries sit at the peak of human spectral sensitivity, and no pure white or black prevents glare and halation.

## Color Palette

- **Pine Green**: `#2D5F4A` — Primary actions, headers. Green requires the least accommodation effort.
- **Soft Pine**: `#4A8B6F` — Secondary actions, hover states. Lighter green variant.
- **Deep Forest**: `#1B3D30` — Dark text on light backgrounds. Green-biased charcoal replaces pure black.
- **Warm Cream**: `#FAF8F4` — Page backgrounds. Suppresses blue spectral energy by ~18% vs pure white.
- **Soft Ivory**: `#FFFEFB` — Card/panel backgrounds. Mimics natural paper.
- **Parchment**: `#F1EDE8` — Alt rows, input backgrounds. Warmer than typical gray.
- **Sand Mist**: `#E2DDD6` — Borders, dividers. High visibility without sharpness.
- **Warm Honey**: `#D4A85C` — Warnings, highlights. Zero blue-light component.
- **Muted Rose**: `#C75D5D` — Danger states. Less saturated to reduce alarm fatigue.
- **Dusty Teal**: `#5B8FA8` — Info elements. Desaturated to minimize blue-light harshness.
- **Charcoal Green**: `#1F2D24` — Body text. 13.1:1 contrast ratio on cream.
- **Warm Slate**: `#6B7B72` — Secondary text. 4.8:1 on cream (WCAG AA).
- **Silver Sage**: `#9CA8A0` — Placeholders, hints. 3.1:1 on cream (AA Large).

## Typography

- **Headers**: Noto Kufi Arabic Bold
- **Body Text**: IBM Plex Sans Arabic

## Best Used For

Educational management dashboards, Arabic RTL interfaces, school administration apps, healthcare/wellness dashboards, productivity tools — any interface used for extended periods where eye comfort is paramount.
