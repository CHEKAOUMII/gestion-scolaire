Access modes
- Guest / blocked:
  - Allowed pages: index.html, students-list.html, settings-imports.html, login.html
  - Sidebar active links: students-list.html, settings-imports.html
- Trial / licensed:
  - All non-admin pages unlocked
  - Admin-only pages remain hidden for non-admin users: settings-users.html, settings-license.html
- Authenticated staff/viewer:
  - All non-admin pages accessible
- Admin:
  - Full access to all pages

Visibility notes
- Hidden by default for non-admin users:

- In sidebar but not in PAGE_VISIBILITY_CATALOG:
