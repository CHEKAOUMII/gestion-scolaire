## Design Context

### Users

Primary users are school administrators and staff in Moroccan qualifying high schools, working in Arabic RTL on Windows desktop machines, often with modest hardware and unreliable or absent internet. They use the app for sensitive, repeat-heavy administrative work: student records, grades, absences, timetables, exams, certificates, setup, backup, and sync. A secondary user is the school owner/operator who manages licensing, deployed devices, and visibility across schools.

### Brand Personality

Trustworthy, calm, official. The interface should create confidence, feel institutionally credible, and lower stress around dense school operations.

### Aesthetic Direction

Light-first Arabic RTL desktop UI with dark support. Borrow the spirit of education administration portals like Noor: formal, structured, familiar, and clear without feeling dated. Preserve the current visual foundation already present in the product: Pencil Blue with warm neutral surfaces, locally bundled `IBM Plex Sans Arabic` for body text, `Noto Kufi Arabic` for display text, and restrained elevated/glass surfaces where they improve hierarchy. Avoid loud consumer-app styling, neon palettes, overly playful visuals, or trendy effects that weaken the official tone.

### Design Principles

- Prioritize operational clarity over novelty; admin tasks should feel quick, stable, and obvious.
- Make Arabic RTL feel native in layout, alignment, spacing, icon direction, and reading flow.
- Keep the visual tone calm and official, using emphasis sparingly and intentionally.
- Meet WCAG AA with strong contrast, keyboard-friendly interactions, reduced-motion-safe behavior, and status signals that never rely on color alone.
- Design for offline-first desktop workflows on modest school hardware, favoring reliability and legibility over decorative complexity.
